# Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 0 — trustworthy foundation

- Shared Claude Code/Codex workflow and architecture documentation
- Reproducible dependency manifests and CI
- Deterministic tests for graph, ingestion, provenance, and research signals
- Truthful market-versus-fixture labeling and timestamps

## Phase 1 — canonical industry data

- Security master and stable issuer/listing identifiers
- SEC EDGAR submissions and XBRL fundamentals
- Versioned raw/canonical/derived data contracts
- Scheduled ingestion, freshness checks, retries, and reconciliation
- Industry overview and company workspaces for the seed universe

## Phase 2 — sourced copilot

- Typed research tools for market series, fundamentals, topology, and documents
- Document retrieval with untrusted-content isolation
- Answer citations, formulas, as-of time, and calculation lineage
- Copilot evaluation suite for factuality and source attribution

## Phase 3 — forecasts and strategy lab

- Point-in-time feature store and model registry
- Calibrated probability forecasts with walk-forward evaluation
- Reproducible backtests with costs, slippage, and capacity assumptions
- Scenario analysis and internal simulated portfolio ledger

## Phase 4 — paper integration

- Explicit revision of the research-only boundary after security review
- Broker-neutral order state machine and pre-trade risk checks
- Human approval, immutable audit log, kill switch, and reconciliation
- Paper-broker adapter; live capital remains out of scope
