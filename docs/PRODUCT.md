# Product Plan of Record

*As of 2026-09. Expect revision; direction over detail.*

## What this is

**The nucleus of knowledge for the AI economy.** A maintained thesis + map +
key-person and money-flow repository for artificial intelligence — for people
who want to sensemake and invest in the AI market **without rebuilding the
tool themselves**. Not a terminal for everything; a complete map of one thing.

Three inseparable parts:
1. **The thesis** — physical supply chains, chokepoints, money loops, the
   bubble question. Opinionated methodology, shown openly.
2. **The living map** — the terminal: graph/map/geo/book views, lenses,
   people, capital flows. Updated when the news breaks; freshness is the product.
3. **The dialogue** — the copilot as catalog interlocutor: ask questions,
   get grounded answers, and (roadmap) formulate reports and strategy
   documents from your questions. This is the defensible niche feature —
   an analyst that knows *this* dataset, not the internet.

## Audience & pricing (hypotheses to test)

**Primary persona — the concentrated prosumer.** Individual investors and
tech professionals with meaningful AI/semis exposure (portfolios or equity
comp), who already read SemiAnalysis-tier content and hold 10+ research tabs.
They buy *time and confidence*, not data. Secondary: junior buy-side /
family-office analysts covering AI without a Bloomberg budget; journalists,
policy, corp-strategy (bubble-sensemaking wedge). Tertiary: institutional
seats. Honest sizing: this niche is thousands of paying subscribers at
maturity, not millions — comps (Doomberg, SemiAnalysis prosumer tiers) prove
four-figure sub counts at premium prices are achievable for singular voices.

**Pricing ladder (anchored to newsletter comps, test everything):**
- **Free** — Pulse summary + read-only map snapshot + methodology. The funnel.
- **Member — $25/mo or $240/yr** — full weekly Pulse, deal-ledger access,
  archive. Newsletter-membership economics; the volume tier.
- **Pro — $60/mo or $600/yr** — the full terminal + copilot (quota) +
  briefs. The product tier; where the tool earns beyond the letter.
- **Founding — $750 one-time, capped (e.g. first 100)** — lifetime Pro.
  The only defensible one-time sale for a freshness product: early cash +
  committed evangelists; cap it because lifetime deals age badly.
- **Institutional — $10–15K/yr** — multi-seat + direct line; sold by hand
  after freshness is proven. Three of these equal ~100 members — pursue
  in parallel, not instead.

Rule: recurring-first. A freshness product sold one-time is a contradiction;
the founding tier is the sole exception and it is marketing, not the model.

## The Pulse (newsletter)

Weekly brief generated from the graph diff + the week's events, hand-edited:
what moved, which money loop grew, who departed where, chokepoint changes.
The newsletter is simultaneously the marketing funnel **and** the freshness
forcing-function **and** the first market test.

## Validation sequence

- **Phase 0 (weeks 1–2):** merge the mega-PR; deploy a read-only demo
  (dashboard degrades gracefully to cached data — no auth needed for a
  snapshot demo); publish the methodology entry page with email capture.
- **Phase 1 (quarter 1): the Pulse.** Free, weekly, copilot-drafted +
  hand-edited. Success gate: does a free artifact this specific attract
  ~500–1,000 subscribers organically? If not, the paid tool won't sell —
  cheapest possible falsification.
- **Phase 2: 10–20 design partners.** Hand-picked ICP users, live access,
  watch them use it, founding-member pricing (or free-for-feedback).
  Metric: unprompted weekly return rate.
- **Phase 3: paid beta.** Requires the launch gates in ENGINEERING.md
  (auth, licensed data, ops). Founding price locked for early subscribers.

Signals that matter: newsletter growth & open rate; demo → return rate;
copilot queries per active user; the Sean Ellis question ("how disappointed
if this disappeared?").

## Copilot-as-analyst roadmap (the niche within the niche)

1. Grounded Q&A over the full catalog — shipped.
2. **Report generation**: "brief me on X" → structured markdown/PDF brief
   composed from tool outputs (topology, quotes, people, deals) with a
   methodology footer. Near-term build.
3. Strategy documents from a dialogue: user's questions + constraints →
   scenario/watchlist documents. Always research framing, never advice —
   the red lines in AGENTS.md apply verbatim.
4. The Pulse pipeline: copilot drafts the weekly diff narrative for human
   edit (curation, not autopilot).

## Standing constraints

Licensing before charging (ENGINEERING.md §2) · people data = public-record
professional facts, sourced, correctable · simulation labeled · no advice,
no order execution · freshness cadence is a staffing commitment, not a wish.
